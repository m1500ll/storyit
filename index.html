Upload once. Post on every account.

Storyit sends each video to all your TikTok and Instagram accounts on a schedule, writes a caption for each account, and never posts the same video twice to the same account.

How it works

  1. Connect your TikTok and Instagram accounts with their official login.
  2. Import your videos once into your library.
  3. Post a video on several accounts at once, or plan a daily rotation.
  4. Approve each caption; the server publishes on time and shows the real status.
